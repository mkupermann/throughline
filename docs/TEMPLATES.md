# AI Team Operations templates

Throughline includes **103 starter templates**: **43 project templates, 15 team templates and 45 role templates**. The domain catalog adds 98 templates across 14 categories to the five general product-improvement starters. These are editable planning blueprints, not claims of professional certification or autonomous execution.

## Start with an outcome

Open **AI Team Operations → Templates**, choose a category and preview a project. Review its objective, stages, deliverables, acceptance criteria and linked team before creating an instance. Creating a project also creates one linked team and its three roles. Assign people, providers, tools and models separately in Resources; templates do not configure credentials, grant tool access or launch agents.

Each project points to a specific team version, and each team points to specific role versions. Editing a template creates a new version. Existing projects retain their original snapshots; their briefs and resources remain editable. To use an updated child template, explicitly update the parent's reference and create a new parent version. Archiving prevents direct new instantiation; already pinned child versions remain reproducible.

## Catalog

Every category includes three project blueprints, one specialized team and three complementary roles. The third role performs independent review. Stages and acceptance criteria are plain-language plans: they do not automatically create tasks, enforce approval gates or constrain a running model.

### Finance

**Team:** Finance planning team. **Roles:** Finance data analyst, Financial scenario modeller, Finance control reviewer.

- **Rolling cash-flow forecast:** Prepare a thirteen-week liquidity view from approved cash, receivables and payables exports. Outputs: Weekly cash schedule; overdue-receivables map; downside scenario; funding decision brief.
- **Budget variance review:** Explain actual versus approved budget and identify accountable follow-up actions. Outputs: Variance bridge; materiality-ranked explanations; owner/action register; management summary.
- **Unit economics assessment:** Evaluate product or customer-segment economics without hiding allocation assumptions. Outputs: Unit-economics workbook; allocation policy; cohort comparison; sensitivity chart.

### Science

**Team:** Reproducible research team. **Roles:** Research evidence curator, Experiment designer, Reproducibility reviewer.

- **Reproducible experiment:** Test a bounded hypothesis against an explicit baseline with reproducible data and code. Outputs: Preregisterable protocol; data provenance; scripts and environment; effect-size report.
- **Structured literature review:** Map published evidence for a narrowly defined question with transparent selection. Outputs: Search strings and dates; inclusion/exclusion ledger; evidence matrix; disagreement and gap map.
- **Dataset quality assessment:** Determine whether a dataset supports its intended scientific analysis. Outputs: Data dictionary; quality checks; leakage report; subgroup coverage; fitness-for-use memo.

### Education

**Team:** Learning design team. **Roles:** Learning needs analyst, Instructional designer, Assessment accessibility reviewer.

- **Course module design:** Create a teachable module with aligned outcomes, practice and assessment. Outputs: Module plan; worked examples; differentiated exercises; rubric; accessible handouts.
- **Assessment and feedback pack:** Prepare fair assessment materials and actionable feedback for a specified course outcome. Outputs: Assessment blueprint; question set; annotated answer key; feedback bank.
- **Learning support intervention:** Plan a small evidence-informed intervention for an identified learning difficulty. Outputs: Skill-gap map; practice schedule; progress measures; teacher/learner reflection prompts.

### Enterprise

**Team:** Enterprise transformation team. **Roles:** Enterprise discovery analyst, Transformation planner, Architecture governance reviewer.

- **Application portfolio review:** Create an evidence-backed retain, modernize or retire proposal for a defined application portfolio. Outputs: Application scorecards; dependency graph; disposition proposals; sequenced roadmap.
- **Enterprise AI pilot:** Define a bounded AI pilot with measurable value, data boundaries and a stop decision. Outputs: Pilot charter; data-flow map; evaluation dataset plan; cost envelope; go/no-go rubric.
- **Change readiness assessment:** Assess whether a proposed enterprise change has owners, capacity and adoption support. Outputs: Impact map; readiness scorecard; communications draft; training plan; adoption measures.

### Mid-size business

**Team:** Mid-market improvement team. **Roles:** Business process analyst, Practical improvement planner, Business outcome reviewer.

- **Sales-to-delivery handoff:** Reduce lost requirements and delays between sales commitments and service delivery. Outputs: Handoff brief; responsibility matrix; exception path; cycle-time baseline.
- **ERP selection brief:** Prepare a comparable requirements and vendor-evaluation pack before procurement. Outputs: Prioritized requirements; weighted scorecard; demo scenarios; migration risk list.
- **Capacity and hiring plan:** Compare workload, capacity and process alternatives before adding headcount. Outputs: Demand/capacity model; skill-gap map; workload scenarios; hiring decision brief.

### Startups

## Adapt a blueprint responsibly

1. Replace assumed scope with your actual objective, inputs, constraints and accountable human owner.
2. Confirm access and data handling before providing source material. Use de-identified examples where appropriate.
3. Choose measurable acceptance criteria and explicit stop conditions before work begins.
4. Assign an independent reviewer and confirm what requires human authorization.
5. Record findings, sources and limitations in the project brief and review artifacts.

Finance, legal/compliance and healthcare templates prepare analysis and review packets. Qualified professionals must approve domain conclusions before reliance. They do not authorize trades, filings, treatment decisions, payments, publication or production changes. Other templates likewise require owner approval for consequential external actions. The application stores these requirements as instructions; users and executor integrations must enforce them.

## Technical contract and upgrades

`content.category` contains the stable category slug. `content.catalog_key` identifies each catalog starter independently of its display name. Project content uses `objective`, `stages`, `deliverables`, `acceptance_criteria` and `team_template`; team content uses `workflow`, `review_policy` and `role_templates`; roles use `instructions`, `expected_output` and `allowed_tools`.

Migration `017_domain_template_catalog.sql` seeds the catalog additively in one transaction and records immutable version-1 snapshots. It never edits earlier snapshots or user-created templates. Upgrades use `throughline migrate`; migrations run once through the migration ledger. Do not manually execute the seed SQL repeatedly. The migration ships in installed packages; `sql/schema.sql` mirrors it for schema inspection and integration tests.
